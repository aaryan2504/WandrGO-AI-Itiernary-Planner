About WandrGo AI Travel Planner

We built WandrGo to turn hours of scattered travel research into a clean, realistic, INR-budgeted itinerary generated in seconds.

Our Mission: Stress-Free, Transparent Trip Planning

Most travellers spend days comparing blogs, spreadsheets, maps, and currency converters before finalizing a single route. WandrGo AI travel planner eliminates that friction by translating your exact destination, travel dates, group profile, dietary preferences, and budget into a structured day-by-day schedule.

Whether you are planning a weekend getaway to Jaipur, a five-day cultural exploration in Kyoto, or a two-week family holiday across Kerala, WandrGo organizes morning-to-evening activities, transit notes, visa complexity indicators, and local dining spots without repeating places across days.

How Our Technology Works

WandrGo runs on a fast Python and FastAPI architecture that supports both cloud generative AI providers (Google Gemini and Groq) and local open-weights models via Ollama. Rather than outputting unstructured paragraphs, our engine enforces a multi-stage JSON schema and deduplication pipeline so every generated itinerary is consistent, readable, and ready to use.

Built for Indian & Global Travellers

All cost estimates default to Indian Rupees (INR / ₹) with separate rows for flights, stays, transport, activities, and daily average spend.

Trip-Aware Multi-Turn Assistant

Your generated itinerary feeds directly into our conversational AI Assistant so follow-up questions reflect your exact trip length, city, and budget.

Why Search Clarity & Accessibility Matter to Us

Great travel software is only useful when travellers can find it easily and load it quickly on mobile connections. That is why WandrGo follows clean semantic HTML, lightweight WebP graphics, structured Schema.org markup, and fast server-side compression.
