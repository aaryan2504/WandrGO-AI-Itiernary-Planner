Why Use an AI Travel Itinerary Planner?

Planning a multi-day trip manually means juggling dozens of browser tabs for transit routes, attraction timings, visa policies, and fluctuating hotel budgets. The WandrGo AI travel itinerary planner brings those pieces together in a single structured workflow. By combining zero-shot prompt engineering with strict place deduplication, WandrGo ensures every day of your trip features distinct attractions, realistic local dining, and transparent estimates in Indian Rupees (INR).

Zero-Duplicate Daily Plans

Unlike generic chat prompts that repeat famous landmarks, WandrGo checks activities across every day so your schedule stays fresh from Day 1 through departure.

Realistic INR Trip Budgeting

Get clear cost breakdowns covering flights, stays, local transit, meals, and entry fees tailored to backpacker, mid-range, or luxury travel styles.

Context-Aware AI Travel Chat

Once your plan is ready, switch to the AI Assistant tab to ask follow-up questions about packing, safety, hidden gems, or budget adjustments for your exact destination.

How WandrGo Builds Your Personalized Itinerary

Every itinerary begins with your travel constraints: origin, destination, trip duration, group size, dietary preferences, and travel pace. Our FastAPI backend constructs a structured JSON specification and validates each activity and restaurant entry before rendering it cleanly in your browser. Want to dive deeper into prompt design and smart budgeting? Read our step-by-step guide on how to plan a trip with AI, or learn more about the WandrGo platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WandrGo free to use? Yes. WandrGo works with free cloud LLM tiers (Gemini / Groq) as well as local Ollama models so travellers can build unlimited itineraries without subscription paywalls.

Can I customize dietary needs and travel pace? Yes. You can set vegetarian, vegan, or allergy notes alongside relaxed, moderate, or action-packed daily pacing before generating your itinerary.
